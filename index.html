Built for creators who stay in control

Four pillars — voice, music, characters, and local-first privacy — in one desktop studio.

Voice

Clone singing and speaking voices for covers, narration, and audiobooks. Keep takes on disk — your samples never leave the studio.

Music

Generate and shape tracks locally. Sketch ideas, iterate on arrangements, and stay in a flow that doesn’t depend on a subscription quota.

Characters

Create consistent faces and looks for music videos and short-form reels. Same character across scenes, takes, and platforms.

Local

Runs on your hardware. Models and projects stay under your roof — ideal when privacy, offline work, or full ownership matter.

How it works

From install to finished clip — without a cloud account in the middle.

Install the studio

Clone or download OpenFabric Studio from GitHub and set it up on your machine. No SaaS signup required.

Bring your materials

Load voice references, music ideas, and character references. Everything stays in your local project folders.

Ship the story

Generate singing or talking takes, score the track, lock the character look, then export for music videos and reels.
